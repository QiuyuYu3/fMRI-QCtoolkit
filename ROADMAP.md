# Roadmap

Features under consideration for future releases. None of them is scheduled yet.

## Automatic rating

A command that rates runs from their quantitative metrics and a lab's QC policy, and passes the uncertain ones to a human rater.

## Structural MRI

Rate structural outputs on their own, starting with cortical surface reconstructions from FreeSurfer or FastSurfer, and show these ratings in the dashboard.

## Diffusion MRI

Rate diffusion MRI preprocessing reports and bring their ratings and quantitative metrics into the dashboard.
