# Citation and references

A paper describing fMRI-QCtoolkit is in preparation. Until it is published, cite the software itself; the repository's [CITATION.cff](https://github.com/QiuyuYu3/fMRI-QCtoolkit/blob/main/CITATION.cff) holds the details, and GitHub's "Cite this repository" button formats them.

fMRI-QCtoolkit is released under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/QiuyuYu3/fMRI-QCtoolkit/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Tools the toolkit works with

- Cox, R. W. (1996). AFNI: software for analysis and visualization of functional magnetic resonance neuroimages. *Computers and Biomedical Research*, 29(3), 162–173. <https://doi.org/10.1006/cbmr.1996.0014>
- Cox, R. W., & Hyde, J. S. (1997). Software tools for analysis and visualization of fMRI data. *NMR in Biomedicine*, 10(4-5), 171–178. <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9430344/>
- Esteban, O., Birman, D., Schaer, M., Koyejo, O. O., Poldrack, R. A., & Gorgolewski, K. J. (2017). MRIQC: Advancing the automatic prediction of image quality in MRI from unseen sites. *PLoS ONE*, 12(9), e0184661. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0184661>
- Esteban, O., Ciric, R., Finc, K., et al. (2020). Analysis of task-based functional MRI data preprocessed with fMRIPrep. *Nature Protocols*, 15, 2186–2202. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-020-0327-3>
- Reynolds, R. C., Taylor, P. A., & Glen, D. R. (2023). Quality control practices in FMRI analysis: Philosophy, methods and examples using AFNI. *Frontiers in Neuroscience*, 16, 1073800. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.1073800>
