# Training materials

The repository's [training_materials](https://github.com/QiuyuYu3/fMRI-QCtoolkit/tree/main/training_materials) folder holds a QC reference sheet for the AFNI pipeline ([AFNI_QC_criteria.pdf](https://github.com/QiuyuYu3/fMRI-QCtoolkit/blob/main/training_materials/AFNI_QC_criteria.pdf)), compiled from Reynolds et al. (2023) and Taylor et al. (2024). For each stage of the processing and each block of the APQC report, it lists what to inspect, with example images and the default thresholds of the quantitative summary. It can serve as a starting point for training new raters and for a lab's own rating protocol. The sheet is still being revised.

## References

Reynolds, R. C., Taylor, P. A., & Glen, D. R. (2023). Quality control practices in FMRI analysis: Philosophy, methods and examples using AFNI. *Frontiers in Neuroscience*, 16, 1073800. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2022.1073800>

Taylor, P. A., Glen, D. R., Chen, G., Cox, R. W., Hanayik, T., Rorden, C., Nielson, D. M., Rajendra, J. K., & Reynolds, R. C. (2024). A set of FMRI quality control tools in AFNI: Systematic, in-depth and interactive QC with afni_proc.py and more. *Imaging Neuroscience*, 2, 1–39. <https://doi.org/10.1162/imag_a_00246>
